What's going on with your system?

Pick the one that sounds like your problem, then call. We'll talk it through on the phone.

  • No heat

    Furnace or heat pump running but the house stays cold, or not running at all.

  • No cooling

    AC running but not cooling, or not turning on.

  • Furnace won't ignite

    Gas furnace or pilot light won't light.

  • Refrigerant leak

    Hissing, ice on the lines, or cooling that keeps getting weaker.

  • Burning smell or electrical issue

    Burning odor from the vents, tripping breakers, or failing parts.

  • Poor airflow

    Weak air from the vents, or some rooms much hotter or colder than others.

Smell gas? Leave the house first, then call your gas utility or 911 from outside.
